NoSleep is ready 🎉

Press ⌃⌘S to toggle Sleep / No-Sleep

It just opened and is waiting in your menu bar — look for the S icon at the top-right of your screen.

That one shortcut — ⌃⌘S (Control-Command-S) — flips keep-awake on and off from anywhere. When it’s on, the menu bar S shows a line through it. You can also click the icon to toggle.

Enjoy — and remember to give your Mac some airflow when you keep it awake with the lid closed.
