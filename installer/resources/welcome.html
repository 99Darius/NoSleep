Keep your Mac awake — even with the lid closed

NoSleep is a tiny menu bar app that stops your Mac from sleeping whenever you want, so music, downloads, and long tasks keep going.

  • Flip it on from the menu bar or with ⌃⌘S.
  • Close the lid and your Mac stays awake — no external display needed.
  • Turn it off anytime to get normal sleep back.

⌃⌘S — the one shortcut to remember. Press it anywhere to toggle Sleep / No-Sleep.

When you click Install, NoSleep is added to your Applications and opens automatically. The first time you turn it on, macOS will ask for your password to allow the lid-closed mode — that’s expected.
