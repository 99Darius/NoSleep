Keep your Mac awake

A tiny menu bar app that stops your Mac from sleeping — even with the lid closed. Music, downloads, and long tasks keep going.

Drop your email to get the installer and occasional update news — no spam.
Free & open-source · Apple Silicon & Intel · Toggle with ⌃⌘S Open source on GitHub →

What it does

⌨️
One shortcut

Press ⌃⌘S anywhere to flip keep-awake on or off, or click the menu bar icon.

💤
Closed-lid keep-awake

Stays awake even with the lid shut — no external display needed.

⏱️
Timers

Keep awake for 15 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours, then auto-off.

🪶
Lightweight

Native Swift, no Electron. Negligible CPU and memory.

Installing

NoSleep is open-source and isn’t signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS shows a “can’t verify / malware” warning the first time. That’s normal. On macOS Sequoia the old right-click→Open trick no longer works, so use Terminal (once):

  1. Download the installer — it lands in your Downloads folder.
  2. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), then paste and run:
    xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/NoSleep-Installer-1.0.1.pkg
  3. Double-click NoSleep-Installer-1.0.1.pkg — it now opens normally.
  4. Follow the installer — NoSleep launches automatically when done.
If step 2 says “No such xattr”, run xattr -c ~/Downloads/NoSleep-Installer-1.0.1.pkg instead.

Using it

🅢
Toggle

Press ⌃⌘S, or click the menu bar S icon. A line through the S means keep-awake is on.

🔐
One password prompt

The first time you keep the lid closed, macOS asks for your password once. After that it’s silent.

🗑️
Uninstall

Choose “Uninstall NoSleep…” from the menu — it cleans up everything and quits.
